The core idea

Identity suggests a loop. Stats decide how well you execute it.

The current build separates the visual/action preset from editable attributes. The presets below are strong starting shapes, not immutable stat classes.

One shared combat model

Players and mobs use the same pools, recovery, movement, action payment, release snapshot, hit quality, defense, and impact rules.

Resources shape quality

AP is the action gate. Stamina and Focus also affect the quality of physical and resonance execution as their pools run low.

Commitment creates power

Facing, position, timing, stationary casting, and momentum meters matter. Strong results come from creating the right combat state first.

General stats

Six attributes, one shared budget.

Every attribute starts at 1. You distribute 18 additional points for a legal 24-point character. Each attribute currently has a hard ceiling of 20.

STR

Strength

Builds physical force: the base energy behind arrows, blades, bashes, and Fury attacks.

DEX

Dexterity

Improves physical handling, increases movement speed, and contributes to AP recovery.

PERC

Perception

Raises Focus and AP capacity, expands awareness, and controls both default camera distance and maximum zoom-out.

INT

Intelligence

Builds resonance force: the base energy behind bolts, novas, meteors, and magical healing.

END

Endurance

Raises HP, Stamina, Poise, physical soak, resonance stability, and—together with PERC—maximum AP.

SENS

Sensitivity

Improves resonance handling, contributes to AP recovery, and directly scales maintained Bolt and Support pumping.

The important pairings: maximum AP comes from END + PERC; AP recovery comes from DEX + SENS; movement speed follows DEX; camera reach follows PERC; stationary maintained payload grows by 10% × SENS of its release-time base payload per second.

HP

Survival. END is its main attribute.

AP

Required to begin actions; refills while you are not running.

Stamina

Physical effort and physical execution quality.

Focus

Resonance effort, precision, and maintenance fuel.

Poise

Impact tolerance; breaking it causes stagger.

Character concepts

Four readable combat identities.

Each preset supplies a visual identity, default attributes, and a current prototype action package. Attributes remain editable on the character-generation screen.

01

Tank

A front-facing controller who turns enemy pressure into Guard Mass, then spends the positional advantage on control, protection, and team setup.

STR 7 · DEX 1 · PERC 7 · INT 1 · END 7 · SENS 1

Signature loop

Face the threat, block or advance deliberately, and keep enemies in front. Frontal blocks and successful control build Guard Mass. Running, rear pressure, missed heavy control, and stagger erode it. More Mass strengthens the active guard and improves key control actions.

  • StabReliable narrow melee pressure and a small Guard Mass gain on contact.
  • BashA frontal knockback, slow, stagger, and taunt. Guard Mass expands and strengthens it.
  • ShockwaveInstant nearby taunt and area control; rewards catching several enemies.
  • HookA fast narrow projectile that pulls, slows, staggers, and taunts one visible target.
  • PotionAn unlimited 0.9-second delayed heal with an 8-second cooldown.
02

Archer

A mobile physical damage dealer who earns accuracy and impact by creating a safe firing window, then chooses between one precise line and broad projectile coverage.

STR 7 · DEX 7 · PERC 7 · INT 1 · END 1 · SENS 1

Signature loop

Hold Bow while stationary and outside immediate melee pressure. Concentration takes four seconds to fill, narrows the cone, and becomes a parallel precision corridor after 60%. Moving, dodging, nearby pressure, or hostile interruption ruins the prepared shot.

  • BowCharge-and-release shot; charge improves force, quality, speed, and aim precision.
  • Arrow ShowerChoose a ground point for a delayed physical area impact with visible travel.
  • FanFires seven real arrows across a compact spread; coverage comes from projectiles, not a fake cone hitbox.
  • PinA focused projectile that heavily slows its enemy target.
  • SupportAn emergency ally-healing projectile; it can maintain while stationary, but the default Archer has low SENS.
  • PotionThe shared delayed self-heal and cooldown safety tool.
03

Mage

A fragile resonance specialist who decides whether to keep moving or anchor in place and turn projectile flight time into damage or healing.

STR 1 · DEX 1 · PERC 7 · INT 7 · END 1 · SENS 7

Signature loop

Release Bolt or Support, then simply remain stationary—no button hold is needed. The projectile automatically pumps by 10% × SENS of its own release-time base payload each second and drains 5.25 Focus per second. Movement, dodge, guard, damage, another action, or empty Focus breaks maintenance permanently for that projectile.

  • Bolt / ResonanceA soft-guided damaging projectile whose payload grows during stationary maintenance.
  • NovaInstant point-blank resonance area damage; strongest near the caster.
  • MeteorPlace a delayed high-impact resonance area inside line of sight, with visible incoming travel.
  • SupportA soft-guided ally heal. It starts at 60% of its unpumped release payload and uses the same SENS pump rule.
  • Self HealConverts a resonance release into immediate personal healing.
  • PotionA separate delayed percentage-or-flat heal on cooldown.
04

Fury

A tempo melee damage dealer who stays alive by keeping a damaging-contact chain alive—not by standing still and trading like a conventional tank.

STR 7 · DEX 7 · PERC 5 · INT 1 · END 3 · SENS 1

Signature loop

Every damaging player contact adds Fury. After a one-second grace period it decays over time. Higher Fury improves the power and AP efficiency of Fury-tagged attacks and supplies scaling damage reduction. Fury Strike accepts repeats at a 30%-shorter interval when AP allows, making sustained contact the heart of the preset.

  • Fury StrikeFast targeted melee pressure with a small forward close; the chain-building basic action.
  • Deadly OneA narrow, high-commitment single-target finishing strike powered by current Fury.
  • Deadly AOEA circular finishing sweep whose reach grows with current Fury.
  • DashA short directional rush that stops and strikes on first body contact; if clear, it ends in a free Fury Strike.
  • PotionThe common delayed recovery tool for resetting between dangerous engagements.

The current PvE run

One choice, one corridor, one boss.

Both enemy cohorts exist from the start. Entering a corridor commits the session, wakes that route, and leaves the other route dormant.

ROUTE A

SWARM

Many weaker mobs. Tests area control, projectile coverage, positioning, and the team's ability to avoid being surrounded.

ROUTE B

ELITE

Fewer stronger mobs. Tests control timing, focused damage, resource discipline, and surviving heavier individual contacts.

FINISH

Gate Warden

Cleaning the selected route opens the boss chamber. Defeat the Warden to complete the prototype run.

Enemy math

Mobs follow the same rules, with sturdier budgets.

Mob 1 is the physical damage dealer, Mob 2 the Tank, and Mob 3 the Mage. Common mobs use 21 free points / 27 total—three more than players, placed into END. Specialist and boss tiers add further END or a DEX/END package, while their outgoing PvE impact is separately scaled for the encounter tier.

Prototype scope: session combat, route choice, boss, death, and respawn. Persistence, economy, gear drops, monetization, and permanent power are intentionally outside this release. Names, values, and balance remain work in progress.
